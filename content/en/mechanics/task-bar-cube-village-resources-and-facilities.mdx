## Village resources at a glance

Your village gives your heroes and pets ways to grow while the idle RPG runs alongside your work. According to the [official Task Bar Cube Steam information](https://store.steampowered.com/api/appdetails?appids=5084200&l=english), hunting, farming, and mining gather resources; the merchant trades ores for rewards; and the library and animal shelter provide boosts. The official description does not specify gathering rates, resource costs, or the exact bonuses those facilities grant.

## How do you gather resources?

Task Bar Cube names three resource-gathering activities:

- **Hunting**
- **Farming**
- **Mining**

The official description says you can gather resources through these activities while you work. It does not identify every resource each activity produces or explain how to start or upgrade them, so there is no verified gathering route to recommend.

## What do the village facilities do?

The **merchant** lets you exchange ores for rewards. The **library** and **animal shelter** are described as boosting heroes and pets; the official overview does not assign a specific bonus to either facility.

Those are the confirmed roles, not a complete facility guide. The source does not list the merchant’s offers, ore exchange rates, or the specific effects of either boost.

## How does the village fit into progression?

The village sits alongside Task Bar Cube’s gear and team-building systems. The official description also lists item fusion, gem socketing, and rarity and tier upgrades as ways to improve gear. It does not establish which village resources, if any, those upgrades require.

For now, the practical distinction is simple: use hunting, farming, and mining as gathering activities; look to the merchant for ore trades; and treat the library and animal shelter as hero- and pet-boosting facilities. Check the in-game descriptions before committing resources, since the official overview does not provide costs or outcomes.
