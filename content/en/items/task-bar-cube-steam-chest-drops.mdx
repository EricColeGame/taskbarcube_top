## How Do Steam Chest Drops Work?

A Steam chest in Task Bar Cube can contain an item the game says is sellable on the Steam Community Market. That makes chest drops worth understanding, but the published percentages describe **chest levels**, not the odds of specific items inside them. According to the [official Task Bar Cube Steam listing](https://store.steampowered.com/api/appdetails?appids=5084200&l=english), players receive a Steam chest for every five minutes of play.

The listing does not explain whether time spent away from the game counts toward that five-minute interval. It also does not specify when or how a chest is delivered.

## What Are the Chest Level Drop Rates?

The official listing gives these odds for each Steam chest level:

| Chest level | Listed drop rate |
| --- | ---: |
| Lv. 1 | 85% |
| Lv. 2 | 10% |
| Lv. 3 | 3% |
| Lv. 4 | 1.5% |
| Lv. 5 | 0.5% |

These figures add up to 100%. They tell you how likely each **chest level** is, but they do not establish what a given chest contains or how often any particular item appears.

## Can You Sell Items From Steam Chests?

The Steam listing states that every item inside a Steam chest can be sold on the Steam Community Market. It does not provide item prices, expected sale proceeds, or a list of possible contents. A marketable drop therefore should not be treated as a guaranteed amount of earnings.

Task Bar Cube also advertises collectible gear with seven rarities and seven tiers per item. The listing does not say how those gear rarities or tiers correspond to the five Steam chest levels, so they should not be treated as the same scale.

## What Remains Unconfirmed?

The official information is enough to establish the five-minute play interval and the chest-level odds. It does **not** establish:

- The item pool or item-specific drop rates for each chest level
- Whether offline time contributes to Steam chest drops
- Whether chest contents have any restrictions beyond those described on the listing

Until those details are published, the chest-level table is the most precise drop-rate information available from the cited source.

## FAQ

### How often do Task Bar Cube Steam chest drops occur?

The official Steam listing says players get one Steam chest for every five minutes of play. It does not clarify whether offline time counts.

### What is the rarest listed Steam chest level?

Lv. 5 has the lowest published drop rate at 0.5%. That percentage applies to the chest level, not to an individual item.

Yes. The official listing says every item inside a Steam chest can be sold on the Steam Community Market. It does not state what those items will sell for.
