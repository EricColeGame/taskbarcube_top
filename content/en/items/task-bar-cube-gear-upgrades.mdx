## How do gear upgrades work in Task Bar Cube?

Gear has several ways to grow in Task Bar Cube, but the official description does not explain every step. It confirms seven rarities, from Common to Divine, and seven tiers for each item. It also names fusion, gem socketing, and rarity and tier upgrades as ways to improve gear.

That gives you a useful map of the systems without requiring guesswork about materials, costs, or the best upgrade order.

## Confirmed gear upgrade options

The [official Task Bar Cube Steam listing](https://store.steampowered.com/api/appdetails?appids=5084200&l=english) describes four gear-related options:

- **Fusion** combines items to produce new gear.
- **Gem socketing** adds gems for extra stats.
- **Rarity upgrades** improve an item's rarity.
- **Tier upgrades** advance an item through its tiers.

The listing does not say which items can be fused, how sockets are obtained, or what an individual upgrade requires. Check the available options in-game before committing gear or resources.

## Gear upgrades versus Cube upgrades

The listing also mentions *Cube upgrades*, but presents them as a separate progression system with hundreds of upgrades and Prestige. Do not assume a Cube upgrade changes an item's rarity, tier, or sockets.

Likewise, the listed Steam chests have their own drop rates. Those rates describe chest levels, not your odds of getting a particular gear rarity or succeeding at an upgrade.

### How many gear rarities are there?

The official listing confirms seven, ranging from Common to Divine. It does not name the five rarities between them.

### How many tiers does each item have?

Each item has seven tiers, according to the official listing.

### Can you add stats to gear with gems?

Yes. Gem socketing is listed as a way to add extra stats, though the listing does not specify gem types, socket limits, or stat values.

### What is the best order for Task Bar Cube gear upgrades?

The available official information does not establish one. Fusion, socketing, and rarity and tier upgrades are confirmed, but their costs and outcomes are not detailed enough to recommend a reliable order.
