Task Bar Cube has no level cap, but that does not tell you when to Prestige. The [official Task Bar Cube Steam listing](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5084200/) confirms that Cube upgrades include Prestige and that ACT League bonuses survive it. It does not explain the reset requirements, rewards, or what else carries over, so a precise Prestige route is not yet supported by the available information.

## How Leveling Works

Task Bar Cube describes its levels as endless, with no stated cap. Heroes fight automatically, including while you are away, making leveling part of the game's idle progression. The official listing does not specify experience rates, level milestones, or whether Prestige changes your level.

Levels are only one part of progression. The listing also identifies hero skills, talent trees, gear upgrades, and Cube upgrades as ways to develop your team. It does not establish which of these offers the fastest leveling path.

## What Prestige Confirms

Prestige is part of the Cube upgrade system, which the official listing says contains hundreds of upgrades without a cap. Beyond that, its mechanics remain unspecified in the available source.

Before choosing Prestige in-game, check the confirmation screen for what it says you will lose and gain. Do not assume that hero levels, gear, talents, or Cube upgrades reset or remain intact: the official description does not answer those questions.

## What Lasts After Prestige

ACT League bonuses are the one persistence rule the official listing states explicitly. You earn them by defeating rival bosses, and those bonuses remain after Prestige.

That makes ACT League relevant when weighing a reset, but the listing provides no bonus values or guidance on how far to advance before using Prestige. It also does not say whether any other progress persists.

## Planning Your Progression

Use the confirmed mechanics as a checklist, not as a fixed build order:

- Keep advancing levels without planning around a stated level cap.
- Review Cube upgrades and the in-game Prestige details before resetting.
- Check ACT League progress if you are deciding what permanent bonuses you have earned.
- Treat any claimed “best Prestige level” or exact reset reward as unverified unless you can confirm it in-game.
