## What Is Task Bar Cube?

Task Bar Cube is a desktop idle RPG and clicker designed to run from your taskbar while you work. This Task Bar Cube beginner guide covers the verified basics: your heroes fight automatically, you collect and improve gear, and long-term progression comes from your team, village, cube upgrades, and resources.

The game supports both a compact desktop window and fullscreen play, making it possible to check your progress without committing to constant active play. According to the [official Task Bar Cube Steam listing](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5084200/), the game is available on Windows.

## How to Begin Playing

Start by keeping the game open in its small desktop format or switching to fullscreen when you want a more focused session. Your heroes continue fighting automatically, including while you are away, so the main beginner goal is to return regularly and improve the systems that support your team.

A practical early routine is:

- Let your heroes battle and build progress over time.
- Review the gear you collect.
- Improve equipment when you have suitable materials.
- Gather resources through hunting, farming, and mining.
- Check the merchant for opportunities to trade ores for rewards.
- Develop your heroes, pets, and village as new systems become available.

The available information does not specify an ideal starting team, exact upgrade order, or beginner build. It is therefore best to experiment with the available heroes and strategies rather than follow an unverified fixed progression path.

## Gear, Fusion, and Equipment Progression

Equipment is one of Task Bar Cube’s central progression systems. The game includes seven gear rarities, ranging from Common to Divine, and every item has seven tiers. This gives you several ways to improve equipment over time instead of relying only on finding entirely new items.

The listed gear systems include:

- **Fusion:** Combine items to create new gear.
- **Gem socketing:** Add gems to provide additional stats.
- **Rarity upgrades:** Improve an item’s rarity.
- **Tier upgrades:** Push equipment through its available tiers.

Because the official information does not provide exact material costs or success rules, avoid assuming that every low-level item should immediately be fused or upgraded. Compare the value of your current gear with the resources required, and keep exploring the systems as your inventory grows.

## Resources, Village Systems, and Team Building

Task Bar Cube combines automatic combat with village development. Hunting, farming, and mining provide resources while you work, while the merchant lets you exchange ores for rewards. The Library and Animal Shelter also support your heroes and pets, although the available source does not list their exact bonuses.

Team building includes heroes and pets, with five hero classes described in the official listing. Each class has its own combat skill, and heroes can be shaped through a talent tree and individual hero skills. This suggests that your preferred strategy can influence how you develop the team, but the available evidence does not identify the strongest class combination.

Other long-term progression systems include:

- **Cube upgrades:** Hundreds of upgrades with no stated cap.
- **Prestige:** A progression option listed within cube upgrades.
- **ACT League:** Rival boss battles that provide bonuses retained after Prestige.
- **Story and quests:** Eight story chapters and daily quests.
- **Achievements:** Steam Achievements tied to game progress.

For beginners, the safest approach is to improve the systems you actively use, then revisit your talent and equipment choices as you learn how your selected team performs.

## Steam Chests and Long-Term Play

The official listing states that playing for five minutes awards a Steam chest. Chest contents can be sold on the Steam Community Market, and the listed drop rates are:

- Level 1: 85%
- Level 2: 10%
- Level 3: 3%
- Level 4: 1.5%
- Level 5: 0.5%

These are official listed rates, not a guarantee of receiving a particular item or value. The listing also says that individual items inside the chests can be sold on the Steam Community Market, so market availability and prices may vary.

Task Bar Cube also features a weekly online ranking for players who want a competitive goal. If rankings are not your priority, the idle combat, resource gathering, quests, story chapters, and endless level progression provide other ways to continue playing.

## Task Bar Cube Beginner Guide FAQ

### Is Task Bar Cube an idle game?

Yes. Task Bar Cube is described as a desktop idle RPG and clicker. Heroes fight automatically, including while you are away from the game.

### Can I play Task Bar Cube in a small window?

Yes. The game is designed to live on your taskbar and can be kept open as a small window while you work. Fullscreen play is also supported.

### How do I improve gear in Task Bar Cube?

The verified gear systems include fusion, gem socketing, rarity upgrades, and tier upgrades. The game has seven rarities and seven tiers for each item.

### Does Task Bar Cube have a level cap?

The official listing describes endless levels with no level cap. It also includes cube upgrades with no stated cap and a Prestige system for continued progression.
