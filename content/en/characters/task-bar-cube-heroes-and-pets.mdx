## Heroes and Pets at a Glance

Your Task Bar Cube team keeps fighting while you’re away, so its heroes and pets are central to the idle RPG experience. The [official Task Bar Cube Steam information](https://store.steampowered.com/api/appdetails?appids=5084200&l=english) confirms that you can build a team around your preferred strategy, choose from five hero classes, and improve heroes and pets. It does not name the classes or pets, explain pet abilities, or provide a team-building formula.

## What’s Confirmed About Heroes

Each of the five classes has its own combat skill. Heroes also have skills and a talent tree that let you shape how they develop. The official description does not identify individual skills, talent choices, or which class works best in a particular situation.

Gear offers another way to develop your team. Items have seven rarities, from Common to Divine, and seven tiers. The game also lists item fusion, gem socketing, and rarity and tier upgrades. Those systems are confirmed, but the available information does not establish the best equipment for any hero class.

## What’s Confirmed About Pets

Pets are part of team-building, and the game’s library and animal shelter provide boosts for heroes and pets. Beyond that, the official description leaves important questions open: it does not specify pet names, abilities, acquisition methods, or how many pets can join a team.

That makes a ranked pet list or a recommended hero-pet pairing premature without more detailed evidence.
